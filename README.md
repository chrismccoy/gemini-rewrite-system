# Gemini Rewrite System

Gemini Rewrite System is a self hosted application. It aggregates news from your favorite RSS sources and uses Google's Gemini AI to rewrite, summarize, and transform raw feeds into unique, high quality blog posts. 

## Key Features

*   ** Smart RSS Aggregation**
    Automatically fetch, parse, and combine from multiple RSS feed sources into a single timeline sorted by the latest updates.

*   ** AI Powered Rewriting**
    Integrates directly with Google Gemini (Pro/Flash) to rewrite articles into unique, SEO friendly content with a single click.

*   ** Curator Dashboard**
    An admin interface to manage feeds, review incoming articles.

*   ** Customizable AI Persona**
    Full control over the AI's "System Prompt," allowing you to tweak the tone, style, length, and formatting of the generated content to match your brand's voice.

*   ** Draft & Publish Workflow**
    AI generated content is saved as drafts first, allowing you to review the output before it goes live on your public feed.

*   ** Lightweight**
    Built on a serverless SQLite database with caching, with zero complex database setup required.

*   ** Secure Authentication**
    Protect your curator dashboard and API usage with built in session based authentication.

*   ** Public News Feed**
    Automatically generates a clean, responsive public facing news feed displaying only your curated and rewritten stories.
